Our Venue Partners

Chef's Table is proud to be the exclusive or preferred catering partner at some of the Fargo-Moorhead area's finest event venues.

Exclusive Venue Partners

At these venues, Chef's Table is the exclusive catering provider. When you book one of these spaces, you're automatically working with us.

The Pines Venue

Exclusive catering partner. We offer two unique Signature Pines Antipasto Presentations — The Pines White and The Pines Black — exclusively at this venue. Priced at $900 for a platter feeding roughly 200 guests ($225 per additional 50 guests).

Visit The Pines (opens in new tab)

Drekker Brewhalla

Exclusive catering partner at Fargo's premier craft brewery and large-scale event space. Perfect for corporate events, weddings, and large gatherings in a unique industrial setting.

Visit Brewhalla (opens in new tab)

Riverhaven Events Center

Exclusive catering partner at this beautifully situated event center. Riverhaven offers a stunning backdrop for weddings and special events in the Fargo area.

Visit Riverhaven (opens in new tab)

Sanctuary Events Center

Exclusive catering partner at downtown Fargo's Sanctuary Events Center — a unique and intimate space for creating lasting memories.

Visit Sanctuary (opens in new tab)
